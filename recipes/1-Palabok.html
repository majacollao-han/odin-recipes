PALABOK

Description

Pancit Palabok is a popular Filipino noodle dish made with thin rice noodles covered in a rich shrimp-based sauce. It is commonly topped with shrimp, boiled eggs, crushed chicharon, and spring onions.

Ingredients

  • Rice noodles
  • Shrimp
  • Boiled eggs
  • Crushed chicharon
  • Garlic and onions
  • Fish sauce
  • Annatto powder
  • Spring onions
  • Chicken or shrimp broth

Steps on Cooking

  1. Prepare the rice noodles by soaking them in water if needed. Cook them according to the package instructions until soft and tender. Drain the noodles and set them aside.
  2. Clean the shrimp and remove their shells. Set aside some whole shrimp for the toppings. Prepare the chicken or shrimp broth for the sauce.
  3. Heat a small amount of cooking oil in a pan. Add the minced garlic and chopped onions. Sauté until fragrant and the onions become soft.
  4. Add the shrimp broth to the pan. Mix in the annatto powder until the sauce develops a rich orange color.
  5. Add fish sauce and other seasonings according to your preferred taste. Stir the mixture and allow it to simmer.
  6. Prepare a small amount of flour mixed with water. Gradually pour it into the sauce while stirring continuously until the sauce becomes thick and smooth.
  7. In a separate pan, cook the shrimp until they turn pink and are fully cooked. Set them aside for the toppings.
  8. Place the cooked rice noodles on a large serving plate. Pour the prepared sauce evenly over the noodles.
  9. Arrange the cooked shrimp and sliced boiled eggs on top. Add crushed chicharon and chopped spring onions for additional flavor.
  10. Serve the Pancit Palabok while warm. Add calamansi juice if desired.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings
20 minutes 40 minutes 4-5 people
