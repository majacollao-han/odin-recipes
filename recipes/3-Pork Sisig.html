PORK SISIG

Description

Pork Sisig is a popular Filipino dish known for its savory and tangy flavor. It is made with chopped pork mixed with onions, chili peppers, and seasonings. It is commonly served hot with rice.

Ingredients

  • Pork
  • Onions
  • Chili peppers
  • Calamansi juice
  • Soy sauce
  • Mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cooking oil

Steps on Cooking

  1. Wash the pork belly thoroughly and place it in a pot with enough water to cover the meat.
  2. Bring the water to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 30–40 minutes or until the pork becomes tender and fully cooked.
  3. Remove the pork from the pot and let it cool. Pat it dry using paper towels.
  4. Chop the cooked pork belly into small, bite-sized pieces.
  5. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the chopped pork and cook until golden brown and slightly crispy.
  6. Add the minced garlic and chopped onion. Stir and cook until fragrant and softened.
  7. Pour in the soy sauce and calamansi juice. Mix well until the pork absorbs the seasonings.
  8. Add the chopped chili peppers, salt, and ground black pepper. Stir until all ingredients are evenly combined.
  9. Turn off the heat and allow the mixture to cool slightly. Add the mayonnaise and mix well.
  10. Transfer the pork sisig to a serving plate. Garnish with chopped onions or chili peppers and serve hot with steamed rice.

Cooking Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings
20 minutes 45 minutes 3–4 people
