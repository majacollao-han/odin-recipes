PORK SISIG

Description

Pork Sisig is a popular Filipino dish known for its savory and tangy flavor. It is made with chopped pork mixed with onions, chili peppers, and seasonings. It is commonly served hot with rice.

Ingredients

  • Pork
  • Onions
  • Chili peppers
  • Calamansi juice
  • Soy sauce
  • Mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cooking oil

Steps on Cooking

  1. Prepare all the vegetables by washing the mung bean sprouts, carrots, and cabbage thoroughly. Slice the carrots into thin strips and chop the cabbage into small pieces.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and chopped onions. Sauté until fragrant and slightly soft.
  3. Add the ground pork to the pan. Cook while stirring occasionally until the pork is fully cooked.
  4. Add the sliced carrots and chopped cabbage. Stir the ingredients together and cook until the vegetables begin to soften.
  5. Add the mung bean sprouts and mix everything evenly. Avoid overcooking the sprouts so they retain some of their texture.
  6. Season the mixture with salt and pepper. Stir well to distribute the seasonings evenly.
  7. Remove the filling from the pan and allow it to cool before wrapping. Drain any excess liquid to prevent the wrappers from becoming soggy.
  8. Place one lumpia wrapper on a clean, flat surface. Put a suitable amount of vegetable filling near one edge of the wrapper.
  9. Fold the sides of the wrapper inward, then roll it tightly around the filling. Moisten the edge with a little water to seal the wrapper.
  10. Repeat the wrapping process until all the filling has been used.
  11. Heat cooking oil in a frying pan. Carefully place the prepared lumpia into the hot oil and fry until the wrappers become golden brown and crispy.
  12. Remove the cooked lumpia and place them on a plate lined with paper towels to drain excess oil. Serve warm with your preferred dipping sauce.
